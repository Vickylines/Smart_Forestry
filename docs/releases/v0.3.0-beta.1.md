## 中文

智慧林业首轮公开测试版，适用于 Android 10 及以上系统。

### 本轮更新

- APK 不内置识别密钥。设置中填写百度 **API Key** 和 **Secret Key**，支持**保存、验证、移除密钥**；密钥通过 Android Keystore 与 AES-GCM 加密保存在手机，保存后清空输入框。
- 设置中的本机项目、观察记录、照片均可打开。
- 新增项目删除与确认，同时清理关联记录、任务和本机照片。
- 修复部分设备无法启动相机的问题，补上 WebView 进程被回收后的拍摄恢复。
- 移除示例项目、示例入口、“无需电脑在线”和重复说明，精简页面与操作层级。
- 支持离线采集、百度植物识别、人工复核、CSV 和包含原图的 ZIP 导出。
- 关闭发布包的应用与 WebView 调试，更新兼容的依赖安全补丁。

### 安装与测试

下载下方 `zhihuilinye-0.3.0-beta.1.apk`。可使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验文件。现有同签名测试包可覆盖安装；更换签名或卸载前请先导出资料。

已完成领域测试、浏览器回归、模拟识别中断恢复，以及 Pixel 4 XL / Android 16 的密钥保存与移除、拍照回传和保存、照片重新读取、系统 ZIP 导出检查。ZIP 已使用独立读取器验证。

**测试范围：**识别成功、部分失败与继续识别使用模拟响应；真机鉴权验证了无效凭据报错。本轮未消耗真实识别额度，也未评测植物识别准确率。验证密钥不代表账号拥有植物识别权限或剩余额度。

**已知限制：**资料仅存本机；暂无云同步或备份导入。卸载／清除数据会删除本机资料和密钥。识别时保持应用打开，中断后可继续。开发工具链仍有审计告警，详情见[验收记录](https://github.com/Vickylines/Smart_Forestry/blob/v0.3.0-beta.1/docs/Beta1%E9%AA%8C%E6%94%B6%E8%AE%B0%E5%BD%95.md)；开发服务器不包含在 APK 中。

反馈请附机型、系统、复现步骤和预期／实际结果，请勿上传密钥或私人照片。

## English

The first public beta of **Smart Forestry**, for Android 10 and later. The app interface is currently in Chinese.

### Changes

- No identification credentials are bundled in the APK. Enter your Baidu **API Key** and **Secret Key** in Settings. **Save**, **Verify key**, and **Remove key** are available; credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore and AES-GCM, and the input fields are cleared after saving.
- Local projects, observations, and photos in Settings now open their respective views.
- Project deletion includes confirmation and removes associated records, tasks, and local photos.
- Fixed camera launch compatibility on affected devices and added capture recovery after WebView renderer loss.
- Removed sample data, sample actions, the “no computer needs to stay online” text, and redundant interface copy.
- Offline capture, Baidu plant identification, manual review, CSV export, and ZIP export with original photos.
- Disabled application/WebView debugging in the distribution APK and applied compatible dependency security updates.

### Installation and validation

Download `zhihuilinye-0.3.0-beta.1.apk` below. Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify it. Existing test installations with the same signature can be updated in place. Export your data before uninstalling or changing signatures.

Validation covers domain tests, browser regression, simulated interrupted identification/resume, and Pixel 4 XL / Android 16 checks for credential storage/removal, camera return and photo persistence, and Android document export. The exported ZIP was checked using an independent reader.

**Test scope:** successful identification and partial-failure recovery use simulated responses. Native authentication testing verified rejection of invalid credentials. No live identification quota was consumed and no plant-identification accuracy benchmark was performed. Credential verification does not establish plant-service permission or remaining quota.

**Limitations:** data stays on the device; cloud sync and backup import are not available. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local records and keys. Keep the app open during identification; interrupted tasks can resume. Development toolchain audit findings remain; see the [validation record](https://github.com/Vickylines/Smart_Forestry/blob/v0.3.0-beta.1/docs/Beta1%E9%AA%8C%E6%94%B6%E8%AE%B0%E5%BD%95.md). The development server is not bundled in the APK.

Please include your device, Android version, reproduction steps, and expected/actual results when reporting issues. Do not share credentials or private photos.
